Privacy and deleting your data

For all Lucky Roll games by The Mountain Commerce Ltd (UK): Shut the Box, Four in a Row, Sea Battle, Yatzy Offline: Five Dice Game, Kafeneio, Poka, Mancala and Code Breaker.

How to delete your account and data

In the game (immediate): open the game → Settings → Delete my online data → confirm. This deletes your online player account straight away, together with your scores and leaderboard places, your online rating and your player profile for that game. If you signed in with Google, the link to your Google account is deleted too. Do this in each Lucky Roll game you played online.

By email: write to themountaincommerce@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data", the game name(s) and your nickname. We delete your data in all our games within 30 days and reply when it is done.

What is deleted and what is kept

  • Deleted: your player ID, nickname, scores, leaderboard entries, online rating, player profile and the Google sign-in link.
  • Not kept by us: we keep no backups of deleted online data.
  • Stays on your phone: offline progress and settings are stored only on your device and are removed when you uninstall the game or clear its data.
  • Adverts: advertising data is handled by Google AdMob under Google's own policy. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.
